# A new game from power-on to walking in the town (format: runtime/input.h).
# Run with a pinned clock so the frames repeat exactly:
#   python tools/run.py 0 13760 --input runtime/recordings/new-game.txt \
#       --rtc "2005-11-23 12:00:00" --hash build/frames/hashes.txt
# START at 900 opens the title greeting; A every 60 frames from 1000 answers every question
# with its default. The train opens at about 1290. Name entry for the player opens at about
# 2490 and is finished by touches at 2500 (31,128), 2560 (180,110) and 2640 (219,180); the
# town's name entry (about 3600-4440) takes the A presses as letters and is finished by the
# same three touches at 4300-4440. The train arrives in front of the town hall at about 7080,
# the player walks in by itself (8190-8460), and the A presses take the player through the
# town hall: the map of the houses at about 9750, the conversations up to about 11460.
# Then the player is free in the town hall (11520). DOWN from 11500 walks out of the door;
# the town opens at about 11920 in front of the town hall. In the town: DOWN 12200-12350,
# LEFT 12400-12550, RIGHT 12600-12800, UP 12850-12950. X at 13050 opens the menu on its
# map page (the town hall showed the map last), Y at 13230 turns it to the pockets page
# (about 13290), B at 13400 closes it (play again from about 13460). LEFT 13520-13600 and
# DOWN 13620-13680 walk again after the menu.
